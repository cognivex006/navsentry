NavSentry Recorder — scope

Step 1. Choose the navsentry.json of the application you are authorized to test. navsentry init <url> wrote it in your project folder.

Only targets.origins and the authorization flag are read. Nothing else from the file is kept, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Origins in scope

    Step 2. Open one of these origins and press Record in the popup. The browser then asks for access to that one origin only; the recorder never asks for all sites.
